Current documentation prefers `detail.code`; `detail.status` is legacy. Its status table maps authentication to 401 and authorization to 403, so avoid assuming all 401s mean missing Voices access. [Error reference](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/resources/errors)
